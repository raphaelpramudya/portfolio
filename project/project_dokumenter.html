Short Film Dokumenter Kedai Kampioen

A documentary project highlighting the story and journey of Kedai Kampioen, created for the Creative Journey competition by UKWMS, where the film won 3rd place. Served as Director and Editor, overseeing the visual direction, storytelling, and post-production.

Role
Director & Editor
Tools
Adobe Premiere Pro
Duration
2 Weeks
Category
Film & Photo
Proof

Overview

Created a three-minute documentary short film highlighting a small business (UMKM) in Surabaya for the Creative Journey competition by UKWMS. The film explored the business's story, activities, and journey through documentary-style visual storytelling.

Process

  1. Pre-Production

    I conducted a survey to identify MSMEs and created a list of questions.

  2. Production

    conducting a shoot at an MSME (micro, small, and medium enterprise) involving several questions, and capturing B-roll for additional clips

  3. Post Production

    Performed editing, then submitted the work, which secured 3rd place in the competition.

Result

Produced a three-minute documentary by developing the visual storytelling and shaping the final narrative through editing. This project strengthened my skills in documentary filmmaking, storytelling, visual direction, and concise narrative editing.
