SKKA: Satu Keluarga Kagak Apal (Short Movie)

A family-themed fiction film created for the SacraCinema Film Competition organized by the Diocese of Surabaya. Served as Editor, shaping the story through pacing and visual continuity. The film received nominations for Best Male Actor and Best Child Actor.

Role
Editor
Tools
Adobe Premiere Pro
Duration
1 Week
Category
Film & Photo
Source

Overview

Edited a family-themed short film produced by Paroki Santa Maria Annuntiata for SacraCinema 2024, a film competition organized by the Diocese of Surabaya. The film explored family values and relationships through a narrative-driven visual story.

Goal

The goal was to shape the film into an engaging and emotionally resonant story while maintaining strong pacing, continuity, and character development through the editing process.

Result

Edited the film from raw footage into the final narrative, refining pacing, transitions, and visual continuity to strengthen the storytelling. The film was nominated for Best Film and won Best Male Actor and Best Child Actor, demonstrating the impact of the production and its performances.
