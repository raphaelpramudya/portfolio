Rasuna Said - Projection Mapping of National Hero

An animated projection mapping project featuring Rasuna Said as a National Hero, combining historical storytelling with immersive visual elements. Served as an Animator, Technical Team member, and Visual Jockey, contributing to the animation, technical setup, and live visual operation.

Role
Animator, Technical & Visual Jockey
Tools
Adobe After Effects & Resolume Arena
Duration
1 Month
Category
Immersive Technology
Live Demo

Overview

Created a video mapping project featuring an Indonesian National Hero as the central theme, combining storytelling, digital visuals, animation, and live visual performance. The project covered the full creative process, from pre-production and visual development to animation and live projection control using Resolume Arena.

Goal

The goal was to transform a historical figure into an engaging audiovisual experience through video mapping. The project aimed to combine historical storytelling with immersive visuals while developing a structured workflow for producing and presenting projection-based content.

Result

Produced a complete video mapping piece by developing the script and storyboard, generating visual assets with AI, animating the assets, and preparing them for projection. Also operated as the Visual Jockey, managing the visual composition and playback in Resolume Arena during the presentation. This project strengthened skills in visual storytelling, animation, projection mapping, AI-assisted asset creation, and live visual operation.
