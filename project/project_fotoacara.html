TGTS 2026 - TRM Goes To School 2026

A study program outreach initiative involving visits to schools to introduce and promote the program. Served as a Documentation Team member, capturing the activities through photography and videography.

Role
Photographer & Videographer
Duration
2 Days
Category
Film & Photo

Overview

Contributed to the documentation team for TRM Goes To School (TGTS), a study program outreach initiative introducing the Multimedia Engineering Technology program at PENS to high school and equivalent-level students.

Goal

The goal was to support the outreach program by documenting the activities and interactions during school visits, while helping showcase the TRM program to prospective students.

Result

Captured the activities and interactions through photography and videography throughout the school visits. This experience strengthened my skills in visual documentation, event coverage, and working as part of a collaborative media team.
