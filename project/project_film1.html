Cuma Satu Menit? | One Minute Film Competition by Sony Indonesia.

A one-minute short film created for the Sony 1 Minute Challenge, focusing on concise visual storytelling within a limited runtime. Served as Editor, Colorist, and SFX Artist, handling the editing, color grading, and sound effects to enhance the film’s overall impact.

Role
Editor, Colorist & SFX Artist
Tools
Adobe Premiere Pro
Duration
2 Weeks
Category
Film & Photo
Source

Overview

Edited a one-minute short film created for the Sony 1 Minute Challenge, a competition focused on concise visual storytelling within a limited runtime.

Goal

The goal was to communicate the film's story and message effectively within a one-minute duration while maintaining engaging pacing and visual continuity.

Result

Edited the film by structuring the narrative, refining pacing, and maintaining visual continuity to deliver a cohesive story within the time constraint. This project strengthened my skills in storytelling through editing, pacing, and concise visual communication.
