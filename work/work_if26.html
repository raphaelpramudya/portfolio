Immersive Fiesta 2026

An event featuring three main agendas: a 3D Diorama Animation competition, a creative workshop, and a student work exhibition by TRM. The event brought together creativity, learning, and immersive experiences while showcasing the work of Multimedia Engineering Technology students.

  • CategoryEvent
  • RoleCreative Direction
  • Year2026

About this work

The project was created to establish a consistent visual identity and promote the event across multiple media. As the Head of the Creative Division, I oversaw the branding and promotional content, including graphic design, vertical video, and live-streaming visuals such as animated overlays, transitions, and on-screen graphics. The project was particularly interesting because it combined static and motion-based visual elements to create a cohesive and engaging experience both online and throughout the event.
